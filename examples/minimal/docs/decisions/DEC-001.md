# DEC-001: Fixed-step semi-implicit Euler in stdlib Python, atmosphere as its own module
Date: 2026-08-14
Status: accepted
Tier: baseline

## Context
G2 needed an integrator and a language before SL-01 could be planned. The walking
skeleton must validate the architecture, not the numerics.

## Decision
Python with the standard library only. Semi-implicit Euler at a fixed step, bounded
to 0.1 s. The atmosphere is a separate module with its own envelope hazard.

## Alternatives considered
### RK4
Rejected because: higher order buys nothing until the validation suite shows first
order failing a requirement; the convergence check (SIM-VAL-001 class 7) will tell us.
### NumPy
Rejected because: a dependency is baseline, and at this size it adds a change tier
without adding capability.
### Atmosphere inside trajectory
Rejected because: the envelope hazard HZ-001 would then have no contract clause of
its own to point at.

## Consequences
- Makes easy: reading the integrator in one screen; swapping it behind C-101.
- Makes hard: performance at very small dt.
- Locks in: nothing; C-101 is the promise, not the method.

## Reversal
Cost to undo: hours (Internal tier behind the contract).
Would be triggered by: convergence study shows first order cannot meet REQ-002 at usable dt.
